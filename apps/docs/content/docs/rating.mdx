## Parts

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Rating` | Holds the score; radios over the stars. |
| `RatingItem` | One star; `data-filled`/`data-partial`/`data-highlighted` paint it. |

Styling lives in `@hashti/styles` under `.hashti-rating*` — gray empty stars, selection-blue filled and previewed.

## Fractional values

Pass `step={0.5}` to let a score end halfway through a star. The star a score covers part of gets ui-sdk's `data-partial` attribute and a `--rating-fill` variable (0–1) with the covered fraction.

To paint the fraction, render a second copy of the icon inside `RatingItem` with the `hashti-rating-item__fill` class. The stylesheet positions it over the empty star and clips it to `--rating-fill`, so it follows both the chosen score and the hover preview automatically:

```tsx
<Rating aria-label="Rating" step={0.5} defaultValue={3.5}>
  <RatingItem>
    <Star />
    <span className="hashti-rating-item__fill" aria-hidden="true"><Star /></span>
  </RatingItem>
</Rating>
```

Without the fill layer, partial stars keep the empty ink and whole stars fill as before. Read-only averages accept any score — `value={4.2}` fills a fifth of the last star.
