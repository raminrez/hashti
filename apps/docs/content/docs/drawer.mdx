## Parts

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Drawer` | Owns the open state; takes `snapPoints` and `defaultSnapPoint`. |
| `DrawerTrigger` | Opens the drawer. |
| `DrawerBackdrop` | The scrim. |
| `DrawerContent` | The sheet; size it, the drawer places and moves it. |
| `DrawerHandle` | The drag handle; pressing it steps to the next larger snap point, then wraps to the smallest. |
| `DrawerTitle` / `DrawerDescription` / `DrawerClose` | Content and dismissal. |

Styling lives in `@hashti/styles` under `.hashti-drawer*` — a full-travel sheet with 16px corners that flatten against their edge and the modal shadow.

## Snap points

Pass `snapPoints` — fractions of the screen up to `1`, pixels above 1, `"20rem"`, `"50%"`, or `"content"` for the sheet's own size. The drawer opens at the first (or `defaultSnapPoint`) and settles on another after a drag; a fast fling can skip intermediate points.

While the sheet rests on a snap point it reports `data-snap-point` on the content (the value as written, like `0.5`) and `data-expanded` at the largest one. Styles add the handle bar and deepen the shadow while a partial sheet floats over content; the full-height sheet keeps the modal shadow.
